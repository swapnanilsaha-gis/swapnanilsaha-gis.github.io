Predictive Urban Water Stress Mapping & Decentralised Infrastructure Siting

Question
Which parts of Bengaluru will be under severe water stress by 2035, and where should recharge wells, storage tanks and rainwater harvesting go?
Data
11 geospatial variables, including rainfall, temperature, land use, groundwater depth, borewells, pumping stations and water pipelines.
Method
Each driver forecast to 2035 with its own model: rainfall by Prophet, land surface temperature by ARIMA, land use by CA-Markov (92.23% OA) and groundwater depth by OLS. Random Forest and XGBoost then map stress for 2025 and 2035. Sites are allocated by K-Means with K = 3, chosen by Elbow, Silhouette and Davies-Bouldin.
Result
XGBoost outperformed Random Forest at 87.97% overall accuracy and κ 0.819, projecting 35% of Bengaluru under severe water stress by 2035.

Question
How fast are Kargil's glaciers retreating, and which areas face glacial lake outburst flood hazard?
Data
11 geo-environmental parameters: DEM, slope, aspect, TRI, TWI, MNDWI, NDSI, SWI, land use, glacier extent and glacial lakes.
Method
Random Forest classification into a GLOF hazard risk map; glacier extent compared between 2000 and 2024; every glacial lake inventoried and typed.
Result
Hazard map at 82% overall accuracy and κ 0.680. Glacier area fell from 1,584.82 km² to 996.37 km², a loss of 588.45 km² or 37.1%. 579 glacial lakes mapped: periglacial 43.7%, proglacial 36.8%, supraglacial 19.5%.

Question
Which pipeline segments should a gas utility inspect and repair first, and what will it cost?
Data
A 12-layer published asset network: 6,214 pipeline segments over 967 km at 3 pressure levels, plus city gates, storage, metering and pressure-regulating stations, valves, IoT sensors and consumers.
Method
Risk = PoF × CoF across 9 weighted factors, ranking a Top-10 priority list. Sensor alerts fire at a pressure drop of ≥ 20% / 40% and CH₄ of ≥ 2,500 / 5,000 ppm. A 3-view dashboard carries 6 KPIs with filters for 5 zones, 4 materials and 3 network levels.
Result
946 segments flagged High or Critical, 1,006 (16.2%) inspections overdue, a $16.3M five-year maintenance cost, 41 open maintenance requests driven by sensor alerts, and cause analysis of 622 leaks.

Mapping Emergency Service Deserts: A Spatial Intelligence Approach To Emergency Accessibility

Question
Where in Greater Hyderabad are residents farthest from emergency help, once road networks, vulnerability and transit are counted?
Data
A 1 km hexagonal grid over GHMC. Road-network distance to 4 facility types (hospitals, fire, police, PHCs), 2 vulnerability indicators (slums, flood points) and 3 transit modes (bus, metro, railway), from OSM networks and the Hyderabad Metro GTFS feed.
Method
K-Means with K = 4, validated by Elbow and Silhouette and cross-checked with a Gaussian Mixture Model, sorts every hexagon into 4 accessibility tiers.
Result
15% of GHMC is a high-priority emergency service desert. Published as a 12-page ArcGIS Experience Builder application and a ward-wise web dashboard.
